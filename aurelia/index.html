CHAPITRE I // LE MONOLITHE TRANSLUCIDE

Time Suspended in Pure Sapphire.

Born from an uncompromising obsession with visual zero-gravity. Maison Aurelia eliminates the traditional metal case, carving the entire structural housing from a 42-kilogram ingot of pure synthetic corundum. Over 900 hours of ultrasonic diamond-wheel milling reveal an unobstructed view of celestial mechanics in perpetual motion.

CHAPITRE II // LE TOURBILLON CELESTE

Triple-Axis Gravitational Equilibrium.

At the heart of Calibre Æ-01 beats an astronomical triple-axis tourbillon carriage crafted in polished rose gold and blackened grade-5 titanium. Rotating on three independent concentric axes at 18, 42, and 120 seconds, it cancels the pulls of earth's gravity in all three physical dimensions simultaneously.

"We do not measure the passage of seconds; we carve a cathedral for their silence."
CHAPITRE III // LA MATIÈRE STELLAIRE
